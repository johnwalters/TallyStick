# TallyStick Potential Features

*Product-planning candidates · August 24, 2026*

TallyStick has reached a feature-complete checkpoint through core bookkeeping, Profit & Loss reporting, and the first Balance Sheet release. The following candidates are ranked by their potential usefulness to the broadest range of small-business accounting users.

QuickBooks Online availability varies by subscription. Its current feature set is included as a market-reference point, not as a requirement that TallyStick duplicate QuickBooks.

| Priority | Missing TallyStick feature | Why it matters | Does QuickBooks have it? |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Bank and credit-card reconciliation | Compare statement balances, clear transactions, identify outstanding items, save reconciliation reports, and close periods. | **Yes.** It provides statement dates and balances, transaction clearing, differences, and reconciliation reports. [QuickBooks reconciliation](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/statement-reconciliation/reconcile-account-quickbooks-online/L3XzsllsK_US_en_US) |
| 2 | Manual journal entries and adjustments | Support depreciation, loans, corrections, accruals, opening adjustments, and accountant entries unrelated to imported transactions. | **Yes.** Journal entries require balanced debits and credits and can be reviewed through the Journal report. [QuickBooks journal entries](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/accounting-bookkeeping/create-journal-entry-quickbooks-online/L6Bzy9mT9_US_en_US) |
| 3 | Duplicate and overlapping-import detection | Prevent duplicated activity when imported files contain overlapping dates or previously imported transactions. | **Partial.** QuickBooks helps find, exclude, and prevent duplicates, but overlapping bank-feed and manual imports can still create them. [QuickBooks duplicate handling](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/duplicate-transactions/fix-duplicate-transactions-quickbooks-online-bank-feeds/L1fjxl88f_US_en_US) |
| 4 | Receipt and document attachments | Keep receipts, invoices, statements, and supporting evidence with the corresponding transaction. | **Yes.** It supports attachments plus receipt extraction and transaction matching. [QuickBooks receipt capture](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/import-transactions/upload-receipts-bills-quickbooks-online/L862MmZHn_US_en_US) |
| 5 | Invoicing and accounts receivable | Add customers, invoices, partial payments, overdue tracking, reminders, and matching deposits to payments. | **Yes.** It supports invoices, payment status, reminders, partial balances, online payments, and recurring invoices. [QuickBooks invoicing](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/accounting/invoicing/billing/) |
| 6 | Bills and accounts payable | Add vendors, unpaid bills, due dates, partial payments, credits, and payment matching. | **Yes.** It tracks bills, vendors, due dates, partial payments, balances, and imported payment matching. [QuickBooks bill management](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/accounting/manage-bills/) |
| 7 | Statement of Cash Flows | Complete the familiar Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow financial-statement set. | **Yes.** QuickBooks Online provides a customizable Statement of Cash Flows, currently documented as accrual-basis only. [QuickBooks cash-flow statement](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/financial-reports/run-statement-cash-flows/L7f72hT6Q_US_en_US) |
| 8 | Recurring transactions | Create drafts or reminders for rent, subscriptions, loan payments, recurring invoices, and regular adjustments. | **Yes, with limitations.** QuickBooks supports scheduled, reminder, and unscheduled templates; availability and supported transaction types vary. [QuickBooks recurring transactions](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/recurring-transactions/create-recurring-transactions-quickbooks-online/L3WoKX2R8_US_en_US) |
| 9 | Dashboard and period-close checklist | Show unresolved work and prevent accidental changes after a completed month or year. | **Mostly.** QuickBooks has dashboards, reconciliation status, a closing-date book lock, and closing-date exception reports. A structured close checklist varies by product and accountant tooling. [QuickBooks book locking](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/close-books/close-books-quickbooks-online/L59LelyPM_US_en_US) |
| 10 | Direct bank feeds | Automatically download bank and credit-card activity for review and categorization. | **Yes.** Connected accounts automatically download transactions, subject to bank and subscription availability. [QuickBooks bank connections](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/banking/connect-bank-credit-card-accounts-quickbooks/L4yDAHMNH_US_en_US) |

## Recommended sequence

1. Bank and credit-card reconciliation, duplicate detection, and period closing.
2. Manual journal entries and recurring transactions.
3. Receipt and document attachments.
4. Invoicing and accounts receivable.
5. Bills and accounts payable.
6. Statement of Cash Flows and dashboard improvements.

Bank reconciliation is the strongest next feature for improving TallyStick as an accounting application. Invoicing is the strongest candidate if the primary objective changes to attracting a broader group of freelancers and service businesses, but it introduces a substantially larger product boundary.
